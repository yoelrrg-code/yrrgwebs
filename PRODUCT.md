# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are founders, business owners, marketing directors, and agencies in Latin America and the US cross-border market who require a high-authority, high-converting web presence without the latency, vulnerability, or operational overhead of WordPress, Wix, or Webflow.

## Product Purpose

Deliver and maintain bespoke, high-performance "Smart Websites" built on Next.js, replacing fragile CMS installations with edge-deployed code and continuous AI-augmented management where updates are requested via WhatsApp or email and deployed with zero downtime.

## Positioning

Managed Next.js Smart Website System combining Silicon Valley engineering standards (Core Web Vitals 100/100, <0.4s load) with an effortless human-in-the-loop operational model. Clients bypass complex administrative dashboards entirely, requesting edits via voice notes or messages and receiving staging previews in under 30 minutes.

## Operating Context

- **Client Channels**: WhatsApp messaging/audio notes, email dispatch, client support portal (`/portal`), and administrative console (`/admin`).
- **Deployment & Edge**: Next.js App Router deployed on high-speed global Edge network (Vercel) with TLS 1.3 and DDoS mitigation.
- **Data & Integrations**: Direct hookups to WhatsApp business links, CRM routing (HubSpot/email), and MySQL database repository for client sessions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, Tailwind CSS v4, Motion, Lucide icons.
- Strict full-code foundation: no third-party visual page builders or fragile CMS plugins.
- Core route architecture: `/` (home), `/servicios` and `/servicios/[slug]`, `/soluciones/[slug]`, `/como-funciona`, `/precios`, `/clientes`, `/nosotros`, `/contacto`, `/blog`, `/portal`, and `/admin`.
- Mobile-first responsiveness and localized Spanish copywriting tailored for Latin American B2B markets.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name**: YRRG.ai
- **Voice & Tone**: Confident, engineering-driven, transparent, and focused on tangible business conversion and operational speed.
- **Key Proof Metrics**: 100/100 Google PageSpeed, <0.4s LCP, <30 min preview turnarounds, 99.99% uptime SLA.

## Evidence on Hand

- Structured site copy, service breakdowns, interactive WhatsApp simulator scenarios, client case studies, and pricing models in `src/data/site-data.ts`.
- Core branding assets and components in `src/components/ui/Logo.tsx` and layout shells.

## Product Principles

1. **Performance Is Revenue**: Core Web Vitals in the green and instant load speeds are mandatory design criteria, not afterthoughts.
2. **Zero Admin Friction**: The client's interface is their chat app; we eliminate CMS dashboard fatigue completely.
3. **Engineered Authority**: Every screen communicates bespoke technical competence rather than generic template aesthetics.
4. **Verified Before Live**: Every modification passes through preview verification before touching production.
